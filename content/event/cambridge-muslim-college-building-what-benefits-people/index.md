This page is the canonical home for my **Cambridge Muslim College talk on Islamic Computing**: **Building What Benefits People**.

The talk brought together three threads: my own journey across engineering, academia, and Islamic technology; lessons from building Greentech Apps Foundation over the long term; and the bigger question of what Muslims should build as AI changes how people search, learn, and ask.

The central argument was simple: the future of beneficial Muslim technology is not only about making more apps. It is about building trustworthy systems, stronger institutions, research capability, and mission-driven people who can serve with sincerity and ihsan.

Slides are available here:

- [Download the Cambridge Muslim College talk slides (PDF)](/uploads/cambridge-muslim-college-building-what-benefits-people-riasat-islam.pdf)
- [Read the accompanying reflection post](/blog/cambridge-muslim-college-talk-islamic-computing/)

Key themes from the session included:

- serving people through your edge
- building institutions, not only projects
- research as a discipline of listening
- Islamic Computing beyond first-generation apps
- responsible Islamic AI: sources, limits, human review, and scholar guidance
