Sharing the Russell Group’s *Principles on Generative AI in Education*. It’s short, sensible, and covers most of what matters—copyright/attribution, data privacy, bias/inaccuracy, and the exploitation of workers in the Global South.

Two gaps I’d like to see addressed in future updates:

1. **Environmental cost of AI** — energy, compute, water.
2. **Misuse / dual-use risks** — deepfakes, fraud, surveillance, militarisation.

Still, a solid baseline for Higher Education policy and staff development. Worth a read and a share:

👉 https://www.russellgroup.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2025-01/Russell%20Group%20principles%20on%20generative%20AI%20in%20education.pdf
