### 🌍 *“Islam Will Reach Every Horizon”*  

Today I came across something remarkable. While reviewing our donation records at [Greentech Apps Foundation (GTAF)](https://gtaf.org), I noticed a small donation from an island I had never heard of before — **Mayotte**, a tiny French territory off the coast of Africa. *SubḥānAllāh.*  

It reminded me of a beautiful saying of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ:  

> **“This matter (Islam) will reach where the night and the day reach...”**  
> *(Musnad Ahmad, 16957)*  

And Allah ﷻ says in the Qur’an:  

> **“It is He who sent His Messenger with guidance and the religion of truth to make it prevail over all religion...”**  
> *(Surah At-Tawbah, 9:33)*  

For centuries, Muslims have understood this as a hopeful prophecy — that the message of Islam would one day reach every corner of the world. And in our time, we see glimpses of this happening — not through conquest or power, but through compassion, knowledge, and technology.  

A donation from Mayotte might seem tiny in the grand scheme, but it’s a reminder of something far greater: how digital platforms, apps, and sincere intentions can connect hearts across the world.  

At GTAF, our mission has always been *“to bring people closer to Allah through technology.”* Every user, every download, every act of charity — they’re all small yet meaningful contributions to a global story that continues to unfold.  

*SubḥānAllāh.* Truly, **“Islam will reach every horizon.”**
